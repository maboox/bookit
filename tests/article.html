A little room for reading

Reading is a small act of attention. A quiet space helps us follow an idea from its first sentence to its final question. We can pause, collect a thought, and return to it tomorrow.

Make space for an idea

A useful reading habit starts with curiosity, rather than a list of things to finish. Keep a few articles that matter to you, and write down the sentence that makes you look at something differently.

A library is a conversation with your future self. Notes and highlights make it easier to remember why a piece of writing mattered, even when the original page has changed.

Leave yourself a question

What would you like to understand more deeply? A good question can stay open. Give it time, gather another perspective, and revisit your first impression with fresh attention.
